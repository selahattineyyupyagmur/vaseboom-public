Privacy Policy

VaseBoom · Effective 18 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you play VaseBoom, why and how, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Controller

SEYA Venture (the individual developer of VaseBoom; not a company). Contact: seyaventure@gmail.com

2. In short

  • You don't need an account to play. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • There are no ads in the game, and we use no advertising or analytics (tracking) software.
  • Only if you choose to join the leaderboard do your chosen nickname and game progress go to our server.

3. What data is processed

a) Kept only on your phone

Game progress (levels, stars, gold, gems, items you own), settings (language, sound levels) and the list of players you follow. None of this is sent to us. It is removed from your phone when you delete the game.

b) If you join the leaderboard (optional)

  • the name you choose (public; it doesn't have to be your real name) and your username (unique, public),
  • the highest level you have reached and your star count,
  • a random player ID (not linked to your name, your phone or any other account),
  • when your record was created and last updated,
  • a backup of your game progress, if you use the recovery code,
  • a report you file about another player's name (who reported whom, and when).
  • To block fake requests, Google's App Check / Play Integrity service checks that a request to our server comes from the genuine app installed from Google Play. Google performs this check; we only receive a valid / invalid result, never a device identifier.

The server does not store who follows whom: your follow list stays on your phone.

c) Technical connections

To keep the lives timer honest, the game checks the time with Google and Cloudflare servers, and it downloads the floor music from GitHub. Your phone's IP address reaches these services and may be processed in their own logs. We have no access to those logs and do not link them to you.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the leaderboard, following and the progress backup: performance of the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Preventing cheating and abuse, and removing inappropriate names: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Answering your requests and complaints: legitimate interest and the exercise of rights (KVKK Art. 5(2)(e), (f)).

Your data is never used for marketing, advertising or profiling, and is never sold.

5. Processors and transfers

Leaderboard data is stored with Google LLC's Firebase service (servers in the European Union); the music is served by GitHub (GitHub Inc.) and the time check by Google and Cloudflare. As these providers are located abroad, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Your data is shared with no one else, except where the law requires it.

6. Retention

Your leaderboard record is kept until you delete it, or until it has not been updated for 24 months. Reports are kept for at most 6 months. When you choose "Delete my account" in the game, every record of yours on the server (name, username, progress, backup) is deleted; the progress on your phone stays.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority). You can delete your data yourself in the game or write to seyaventure@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. If you are under 13, please don't join the leaderboard. If a child's data has reached us, it is deleted at once at a parent's request.

9. Usernames

Names and usernames are public. Swearing, insults, hate speech and names that impersonate someone else are not allowed; names reported by players may be hidden and removed. We advise you not to use your real name or contact details as your name.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server records are set up so that each player can only write their own record.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
