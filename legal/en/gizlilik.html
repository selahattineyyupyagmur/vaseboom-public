Privacy Policy

VaseBoom · Effective 25 November 2026

This policy explains what personal data is processed when you play VaseBoom, why and how, under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK).

1. Controller

SEYA Venture (the individual developer of VaseBoom; not a company). Contact: seyaventure@gmail.com

2. In short

  • You don't need an account to play. Your progress and settings stay on your phone.
  • We never ask for your name, e-mail, phone number, location, contacts or photos.
  • The game is free and supported by Google AdMob ads: optional rewarded videos and, now and then, a full-screen ad between levels. In Europe you are asked for consent before ads are personalised. We use no analytics (tracking) software. If the game crashes, we receive a technical crash report so we can fix the fault.
  • Only if you choose to join the leaderboard do your chosen nickname and game progress go to our server.

3. What data is processed

a) Kept only on your phone

Game progress (levels, stars, gold, gems, items you own), settings (language, sound levels) and the list of players you follow. None of this is sent to us. It is removed from your phone when you delete the game.

b) If you join the leaderboard (optional)

  • the name you choose (public; it doesn't have to be your real name) and your username (unique, public),
  • the highest level you have reached and your star count,
  • the slingshot and ball look you have equipped, the list of slingshots and ball looks you own, and your daily trophy, achievement and golden floor counts (public, on your profile card),
  • a random player ID (not linked to your name, your phone or any other account),
  • when your record was created and last updated,
  • a backup of your game progress, if you use the recovery code,
  • a report you file about another player's name or photo (who reported whom, and when).
  • If you choose to link your account to Google: your Google account ID and e-mail address are kept by Google's Firebase Authentication for sign-in only; they are never shown to anyone or on the leaderboard. When linking you are asked whether your Google profile photo may appear on the leaderboard (public); you can turn it off at any time in Settings > Account.
  • To block fake requests, Google's App Check / Play Integrity service checks that a request to our server comes from the genuine app installed from Google Play. Google performs this check; we only receive a valid / invalid result, never a device identifier.

The server does not store who follows whom: your follow list stays on your phone.

c) Technical connections

To keep the lives timer honest, the game checks the time with Google and Cloudflare servers, and it downloads the floor music from GitHub. Your phone's IP address reaches these services and may be processed in their own logs. We have no access to those logs and do not link them to you.

d) Crash reports

If the game crashes or hits an error, Google's Firebase Crashlytics service sends us a technical report: the error and where in the code it happened, the game version, the phone model and operating system version, the time of the crash and a random identifier for this installation of the app. The report holds no name, in-game name, location or advertising ID. Reports are deleted after 90 days.

e) Ads (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads served by Google's AdMob:

  • Rewarded videos: watched only if you choose to; watching one to the end gives an in-game reward (a ball, a life, gold, gems, a chest, an item).
  • Ads between levels: only after the first 15 levels, at most once every few levels and at least 2.5 minutes apart, and only when a level has ended.

To show ads, Google processes from your phone: the advertising ID (Android Advertising ID), your IP address and the approximate location derived from it (country/city level), the device model, operating system, language, app information and your interactions with ads (views, clicks). This data goes straight to Google; we have no access to it and only receive total ad earnings and counts. How Google uses this data: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Consent: in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent form at first launch; if you do not allow personalised ads, only non-personalised (contextual) ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > About > Ad preferences. In every country you can delete or reset your advertising ID in your phone's Settings > Google > Ads.

4. Purposes and legal bases

  • Providing the leaderboard, following and the progress backup: performance of the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b); KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Preventing cheating and abuse, and removing inappropriate names: legitimate interest (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Finding and fixing crashes and errors (crash reports): legitimate interest in a game that works (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Answering your requests and complaints: legitimate interest and the exercise of rights (KVKK Art. 5(2)(e), (f)).
  • Showing and measuring ads (so the game can stay free): your consent for personalised ads (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a); KVKK Art. 5(1)), legitimate interest for non-personalised ads (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f); KVKK Art. 5(2)(f)).

Your leaderboard, backup and crash data is never used for marketing, advertising or profiling, and we never sell any of your data. Ad data is processed by Google only, as described in section 3(e).

5. Processors and transfers

Leaderboard data is stored with Google LLC's Firebase service (servers in the European Union), crash reports with Google's Firebase Crashlytics, and ads are served by Google's AdMob; the music is served by GitHub (GitHub Inc.) and the time check by Google and Cloudflare. As these providers are located abroad, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. Your data is shared with no one else, except where the law requires it.

6. Retention

Your leaderboard record is kept until you delete it, or until it has not been updated for 24 months. Reports are kept for at most 6 months. When you choose "Delete my account" in the game, every record of yours on the server (name, username, progress, backup) is deleted; if your account is linked to Google, that link and your e-mail address are deleted from the sign-in records too. The progress on your phone stays.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority). You can delete your data yourself in the game or write to seyaventure@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days.

8. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and its ads are not marked as directed at children. If you are under 13, please don't join the leaderboard. If a child's data has reached us, it is deleted at once at a parent's request.

9. Usernames

Names and usernames are public. Swearing, insults, hate speech and names that impersonate someone else are not allowed; names reported by players may be hidden and removed. We advise you not to use your real name or contact details as your name.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Server records are set up so that each player can only write their own record.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new effective date.
